How to play The Secret Pet

A guide for a Windows computer. Nothing to install, no account and no internet needed once it's downloaded.

Getting it ready (once)

  1. Download the gameClick the download link for Secret-Pet-Windows.zip. It goes into your Downloads folder. If your browser asks whether to keep the file, choose Keep.
  2. Open your Downloads folderClick the yellow folder icon (File Explorer) on the taskbar at the bottom of the screen, then click Downloads on the left.
  3. Unzip itRight-click Secret-Pet-Windows.zip and choose Extract All…, then click Extract. A new window opens with the Secret Pet folder inside.
  4. Move it somewhere safe (recommended)Drag the Secret Pet folder onto Documents on the left of the File Explorer window, so it doesn't get tidied away with your other downloads.

Playing

  1. Open the gameOpen the Secret Pet folder and double-click Play Secret Pet.html. It opens in your web browser (Microsoft Edge or Chrome). It takes a few seconds to load the first time. If Windows asks which app to use, pick Microsoft Edge or Google Chrome.
  2. Click the game onceBrowsers keep pages quiet until you click them, so the first click switches on the sound, the music and the voice that reads everything aloud.
  3. Make it big (optional)Press F11 for full screen. Press F11 again to get out. (On some laptops it's Fn + F11.)
  4. Pick a player and a petTap Aubrey, Charlotte, Ashton or Levi, then tap the unicorn or the baby dinosaur to start the story.
Good to know: the story starts over each time the game is closed, so leave the window open between play sessions if your child is in the middle of an adventure. The game does remember which child was picked last, and whether the music was on.

Controls

Mouse, trackpad or touch screenTap the buttons, cards and things on screen. Most games can be played with just taps.
← → ↑ ↓Walk, steer and choose.
SpaceJump, and move the story on to the next line.
1 2 3 4Pick answer 1, 2, 3 or 4 in the quiz games.
RRead aloud on or off.
MAll sound on or off.
NMusic on or off (sound effects stay on).

Shortcut for next time

Right-click Play Secret Pet.html, choose Show more options (Windows 11 only), then Send to → Desktop (create shortcut). Double-click the new icon on your Desktop whenever you want to play.

If something isn't right

There's no sound or no voice
Click once on the game. Check the computer's volume isn't turned down or muted. Look at the bottom-right corner of the game: if it says sound off press M, and if it says read aloud off press R.
It opened in the wrong app (for example, Notepad)
Right-click Play Secret Pet.html, choose Open with, then pick Microsoft Edge or Google Chrome.
The screen stays dark or says "Loading…" for a long time
Close the window and double-click Play Secret Pet.html again. Make sure you opened the file from the unzipped Secret Pet folder, not from inside the zip.
The voice sounds robotic
The game uses the English voice built into Windows. It's clear, though a little robotic, and there isn't a way to change it from inside the game yet.
